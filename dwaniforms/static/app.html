How it works

  1. Pick a form. Questions come one at a time.
  2. Tap the mic and speak, or type. Your answer is read back: "Is this correct?"
  3. At the end, download or print the filled form or letter.

Your information

Answers are kept only for this session and deleted after 30 minutes. Aadhaar and account numbers are shown masked.
