Privacy Policy

1. What we collect

To create an account, we collect your name, email address, and a hashed password (or your Google account info if you sign up with Google). Live captioning and translation run locally on the presenter's own device during a session, using on-device speech recognition and translation models -- your audio and captions are not sent to DwaniLive's servers.

2. How we use it

Account data is used to authenticate you, manage your subscription and license key, and communicate service-related updates.

3. Sharing

We don't sell personal data. Data may be shared with service providers who help operate DwaniLive (for example, payment processing), under confidentiality obligations.

4. Data retention

Account data is kept for as long as your account is active, or as needed to comply with legal obligations.

5. Your choices

You can update your account details from Settings, or request account deletion by contacting support.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be communicated to account holders.
