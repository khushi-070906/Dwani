Frequently asked

Do I need internet during my talk?

No. You activate once while online (the same moment you download the Whisper/NLLB models). After that, the license is cached on your laptop and every session validates locally, with no network call at the venue.

What happens if my subscription lapses mid-conference?

There's a 7-day grace period built into the license. You'll see a warning, but sessions keep running so you're never stranded mid-event.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Subscriptions are billed and managed through Razorpay, and you can cancel from the confirmation email at any time. Your license stays valid until the end of the current billing period.

Is my audio or transcript sent anywhere?

No. Only the one-time activation call (email + license key) talks to our server. Every conference session itself runs entirely over your local network.
