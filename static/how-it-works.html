How it works

From subscribing to live captions in a room, in five steps

DwaniLive runs on your own laptop during the talk -- so it keeps working even on flaky conference Wi-Fi. Here's the whole flow, start to finish.

1

Subscribe to a plan

Pick Pro or Institution on the pricing page and complete checkout. You'll be asked to create an account (or log in) first, since your subscription is tied to your account.

2

Open your dashboard

Once payment goes through, your dashboard shows your license key and two downloads: the DwaniLive app itself, and a small one-time activation tool.

3

Activate once, while online

Run the activation tool with the command shown on your dashboard (your license key and email are already filled in). It checks your subscription with our server and saves a signed pass on your laptop.

Needs internet -- one time only
4

Start a session before you present

Double-click the DwaniLive app. It checks the pass saved in step 3 (no server call needed), asks for microphone access, and gives you a join link and a QR code to display on your title slide.

Works fully offline from here
5

Present -- captions appear live for the room

As you speak, live captions (translated, if you've set that up) stream to everyone who joined. You can watch how many attendees are connected and see questions they send in, right from your own screen.

What attendees do: nothing to install. They scan the QR code or open the link with their phone's camera or browser, and captions appear immediately in their language. Everything stays on the venue's local network -- no data leaves the building.
